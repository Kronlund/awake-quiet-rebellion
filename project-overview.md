# Project Overview: Awake by Quiet Rebellion

## Concept

**Awake by Quiet Rebellion** is Joshua Kronlund's ongoing creative and professional project at the intersection of AI compliance, cybersecurity, and storytelling.

Core idea: AI governance is too important to stay locked in compliance departments and regulatory documents. It belongs in culture. Comics, visual systems, and interactive tools are how you get there.

## Components

### 1. The Comic Series — "JOSHUA & GROK: AI COMPLIANCE SLAPDOWN!"
- Original comic strips in a vibrant comic-book style
- Protagonist: #1 Joshua — a cartoon version of Joshua Kronlund reflecting his current appearance
- Ally: Sofia Mendes — sharp, capable compliance coordinator (inspired by a real person in Joshua's life)
- Alternate character versions (non-canon): secret identity, superhero transformations, signature mask
- Visual identity: Quiet Rebellion branding — dark blue and gold, numbers 3, 6, 9
- Content: AI compliance concepts dramatized through story — safety, guardrails, accountability, red-teaming, the human side of governance
- Status: Active. Full series outline and reference binder maintained alongside published strips.

### 2. The Quiet Rebellion Weekly War Room
- Custom interactive HTML web app (built via v0.dev)
- Dark tactical theme with glowing accents
- Time-blocked task management, priorities, daily structure
- Built to replace generic planners that didn't fit Joshua's workflow (INTJ, block-based thinking, aversion to cluttered Kanban)
- Status: Deployed and in daily use. Planned enhancements: Pomodoro timer, Kanban boards, richer structure.

### 3. The Brand — Quiet Rebellion
- Vintage-yet-modern identity
- Dark blue and gold palette
- Sacred numbers: 3, 6, 9
- Multiple logo directions explored
- Long-term vision: a training company teaching cybersecurity and AI skills through engaging, gamified methods

## Roadmap
- More comic strips and completed story arcs
- Web-native / interactive versions of stories
- Expanded War Room features
- Public portfolio site tying the brand, comics, and tools together
- Eventually: training content and community built on the same foundation
