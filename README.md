# Awake by Quiet Rebellion

**AI Compliance Comic Series & Portfolio Project**

A comic series and creative portfolio project by Joshua Kronlund, exploring AI compliance, governance, and responsible AI through storytelling.

- **Series:** "JOSHUA & GROK: AI COMPLIANCE SLAPDOWN!"
- **Brand:** Quiet Rebellion (dark blue and gold, numbers 3, 6, 9)
- **Status:** Ongoing — new strips, character development, and reference materials in progress
- **Link:** https://github.com/Kronlund/awake-quiet-rebellion

## What's in this repo

- `statement-of-exceptional-work.md` — the portfolio writeup for job applications
- `project-overview.md` — full project description, characters, and roadmap

## Why this project exists

AI compliance is abstract and jargon-heavy. Most people tune out. Comics don't. This project turns governance frameworks (NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001) into stories people actually want to read — and builds a public, verifiable body of work in the process.
